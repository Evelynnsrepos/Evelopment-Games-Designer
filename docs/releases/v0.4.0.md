## What's new in 0.4.0

**Spell check and AI helper**
- Spell check in English and German in every rich text editor (Writer, Wiki and more). German compound words are understood.
- Right-click an underlined word for suggestions, or add it to your own dictionary. Names of your items, characters, towns and enemies always count as correct.
- Optional AI helper: download it in Settings (about 1.1 GB). It runs on your computer, nothing is sent to the internet, and it picks the best spelling for the sentence.

**Plugins**
- Plugins add new tools. Install one in Settings from a GitHub link or a zip. Every install shows a clear warning, because plugins run with full access to your computer and can affect shared projects.
- Make your own: see [docs/PLUGINS.md](https://github.com/Evelynnsrepos/Evelopment-Games-Designer/blob/main/docs/PLUGINS.md) and the Dice Roller example plugin.

**Projects**
- Export a project as a zip from its menu on the start screen (saved to Downloads unless you choose another place), and bring one back with **Import zip**.

**Workspace**
- Click a tool in the sidebar to show only that tool (the app asks first when several are open; Enter confirms). Shift-click opens it next to the others.
- New **Settings** dialog on the start screen and in the sidebar.
- The window title shows the app version.

**Cosmos Creator**
- Isometric view. The top level is a node graph; galaxies and solar systems show orbits, with moons circling their planets.
- Give any planet, sun or other body your own picture.

**Drawing**
- Pen size and smoothing sliders on every canvas (Moodboard, Brainstorm Board, Map Creator).
