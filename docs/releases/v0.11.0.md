## What's new in 0.11.0: Sketch Pro

The Sketch tool is now a full painting app. Still no generative AI: every stroke is yours.

**Engine**
- Layers are blended on the graphics card, with 26 blend modes (new: Linear burn, Darker and Lighter color, Vivid, Linear and Pin light, Hard mix, Exclusion, Subtract, Divide). Computers without graphics support still work.
- 250 undo steps, canvases up to 8K, and a layer limit that matches your memory.

**Pen and input**
- Pressure, tilt and twist from drawing tablets, the eraser end of the pen, and pen buttons for any action.
- Your own pressure curve, smoothing and motion filtering, a tablet test page, and every shortcut can be changed.
- A round QuickMenu (Q) with six actions you choose, touch gestures, and turning or mirroring the view.

**Brushes**
- About 120 brushes in 14 sets, and a Brush Studio to edit everything: stroke, taper, shape, grain, rendering, wet mix, color dynamics, pressure, tilt and speed curves, dual brushes and size presets, with a drawing pad to try them.
- Make your own shapes and grains, keep reset points, and export brushes as .egdbrush files.
- Import .abr, .brush and .brushset brush files. Imported brushes stay under their creator's license.
- A real Smudge tool.

**Color**
- Disc, classic, harmony, value and palette pickers, a second color and recent colors.
- Palettes from images, and import and export of .swatches, .ase and .aco.
- Drag a color onto the canvas to fill an area.
- Display P3 canvases where your system supports them.

**Layers, selections and transform**
- Groups, layer masks, lock, a reference layer, hide from export, merging several layers, and drag a layer out to save it.
- Magic wand, freehand, polygon, rectangle and ellipse selections, with feathering and saved selections.
- Freeform, distort and warp transforms with snapping.

**Adjustments**
- Hue and saturation, color balance, curves, gradient map, blurs, noise, sharpen, bloom, glitch, halftone and chromatic aberration, on the whole layer or painted in with the pen.
- Liquify and Clone.

**Guides, text and animation**
- Grid, isometric, perspective and symmetry guides (including radial), with Drawing Assist per layer.
- Edit a QuickShape's points after it snaps.
- Editable text layers with font import.
- Animation with onion skin and playback, sketchbook pages, and sending frames or pages to the Storyboard.

**Files**
- Time-lapse recording with replay and video export.
- Import and export PSD with layers, groups and blend modes. Also PNG, JPEG, TIFF, layers as PNGs, animated GIF and PNG, and WebM video.
- Resize, crop and turn the canvas, see canvas info, and open a Reference Companion window.

**Everywhere**
- The Moodboard, Brainstorm and Map pens can draw with the same brushes and pen pressure.
- Drawings now save together with the rest of the project when you close the app.
