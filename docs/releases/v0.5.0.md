## What's new in 0.5.0

**Sketch**: a new drawing and painting tool
- Pressure brushes: Pencil, Ink, Marker, Paint and Airbrush, plus an eraser. With a pen tablet, pressing harder makes lines thicker or stronger.
- Size, opacity and smoothing sliders for every brush; `[` and `]` change the size.
- Layers with opacity, blend modes (Multiply, Screen, Overlay and more), alpha lock, clipping masks, duplicate, merge down and reordering.
- Lasso and rectangle selection; painting, fill, clear, flip and move only affect the selection.
- Mirror drawing (left/right, top/bottom or four ways) and an eyedropper.
- Reference images that float over the canvas, taken from any image in the project.
- Export as PNG, or Send to a Moodboard, a Design Language board or the Asset Pool.

**Design Language**: the look of your game on one board
- Works like the Moodboard: images, cutouts, text, layers, and your Sketch drawings as stickers.

**Moodboard**
- Opens clean like the Brainstorm board. The new Drawings drawer places your Sketch drawings as transparent stickers.

**Asset Pool**: everything that still has to be made
- Assets with kind (icon, sprite, 3D model, sound, music…), status (Needed, In progress, Review, Done), notes and pictures, and a progress bar.
- Add many at once: one for every item, character, town or enemy that doesn't have one yet, or one per line of a pasted checklist.
- Assets made from an item or character follow its name when you rename it.

**Drawing everywhere**
- The pen on Moodboards, Brainstorm Boards and Maps now has an opacity slider next to size and smoothing. Map stamp size is a slider.
