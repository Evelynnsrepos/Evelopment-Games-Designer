## What's new in 0.10.0

The biggest update yet: 23 new tools, genre templates and export to game engines. Still no generative AI: everything in your project comes from you.

**Systems and balancing**
- **Skill Tree**: skill and talent trees with ranks, costs and requirements, plus a build planner that counts your points.
- **Abilities & Spells**: costs, cooldowns and numbers from Damage Calculator formulas.
- **Crafting & Recipes**: ingredients, stations and times, a crafting tree and the raw materials anything needs.
- **Combat Simulator**: two teams fight a thousand times; see win rates, fight length, damage per fighter and an example fight.
- **Economy Simulator**: where your currency comes from and where it goes over hours of play, with purchase times and inflation warnings.
- **Balance Dashboard**: item and enemy numbers that stick out, the enemy difficulty curve and gaps in your data.
- **Gameplay Loop** can now simulate resources per step over many loops.

**World and story**
- **Factions** with leaders, members, towns and a relations matrix from allied to at war.
- **Family Tree** built from your Character List.
- **Names & Languages**: a name generator from a language's sounds and a small made-up language with a dictionary and translator.
- **Calendar & Eras**: your world's months, weekdays and eras, a month view and a date calculator. Timelines show era years.
- **Achievements** with unlock rules, points, rewards, hidden ones and rarity.
- **Level Layout**: paint levels and dungeons on a tile grid with rooms, doors, enemies, loot, spawns and notes.
- **Storyboard**: cutscene frames with shots, camera moves, dialogue and timing, played back as an animatic.
- **UI Flow**: menu and screen mockups in phone, tablet or desktop frames, joined with arrows.
- **Localization**: a string table of every player-facing text in every language, collected from the project, with CSV import and export.

**Planning**
- **Vision & Pillars**: pitch, vision, player fantasy, selling points, design pillars, references and anti-goals on one page.
- **Scope Planner**: features sorted into must, should, could, won't and cut, with effort against the days you have.
- **Task Board**: tasks with pictures, your own columns and categories, linked to the entries they are about.
- **Playtests**: sessions with scores and findings, and every open problem in one place.
- **Pacing Graph**: the player journey as beats on an intensity graph, with pacing advice.
- **Pitch Deck**: slides built from your vision, pillars, characters and items, presented full screen.
- **History**: earlier versions of items, characters, towns and enemies, to compare and restore.

**Everywhere**
- **Genre templates**: start a project as an RPG, action game, roguelike, strategy game, visual novel, gacha or cozy game and get the right tools, a vision page with pillars and a starter task list.
- **Engine export**: send items, characters, towns, enemies, quests, dialogue and translations to Godot 4, Unity or Unreal, or as plain JSON.
- **Layout Mode** (press Esc) now lets you drag tools: drop one in the middle of another to swap them or on an edge to put it beside it, with a preview of where it lands. A button between two tools switches them between side by side and stacked. Layout Mode stays on until you press Esc again.
- **Spreadsheet**: CSV import and export, charts, and frozen rows and columns.
- **Timeline**: a bigger handle and Longer and Shorter buttons make stretching a timeline easy.
- The sidebar's bottom buttons fold away with Show less, and Add tool sits right below your tools.
- **Ask Your Project** (optional, off by default): look things up in your own notes and find contradictions with the local AI helper. It runs only on your computer, works offline, collects nothing and never writes for you. Turn it on in Settings after downloading the AI helper.
