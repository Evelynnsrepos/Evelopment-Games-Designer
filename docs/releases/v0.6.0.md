## What's new in 0.6.0

**Rarities**
- New **Rarities** window (bottom of the sidebar). Rarity comes ready with Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic and Legendary, each with a color, a border (solid, double or glow), an icon and a rating.
- Make it yours: rename, reorder, add or remove rarities, pick colors, use your own picture as an icon, switch borders off, and show the rating as 1 to 5 stars, as a number like 4.5, or not at all.
- Any number of systems: next to Rarity you can add Tier, Quality, Threat level or anything else, each used on the lists you choose (items, characters, towns, enemies).
- Your own fields per system, such as a drop rate, a sell price multiplier or a max level, filled in per rarity and shown in the wiki and on hover.
- Rarities show everywhere: framed cards, colored names in tables, entry pages, the wiki info box and `[[links]]`.

**Working together**
- The person who shares a project is now the host. When the host closes the project or stops sharing, everyone else is disconnected and asked whether to keep their copy as a normal project or delete it.
- Only the host can remove people, and nobody can remove the host. Everyone else gets **Leave project**, keeping or deleting their copy.
- Sketch drawings stay on each computer instead of syncing live. Pictures made from them, like stickers on a Moodboard, still sync.
- Everyone working together needs version 0.6.0.
