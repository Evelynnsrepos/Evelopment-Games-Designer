## What's new in 0.6.0

**Rarities**
- The Rarity category now comes with colors, borders and icons: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic and Legendary. It works on items and characters (and towns and enemies if you switch it on).
- Items and characters show their rarity everywhere: framed cards, colored names in tables, their page, the wiki info box and `[[links]]`.
- Change the looks in **Categories**: a color, a border (solid, double or glow) and an icon per option. Add your own rarities, or tick "Colors, borders and icons" to style any dropdown, like Element.

**Working together**
- The person who shares a project is now the host. When the host closes the project or stops sharing, everyone else is disconnected and asked whether to keep their copy as a normal project or delete it.
- Only the host can remove people, and nobody can remove the host. Everyone else gets **Leave project**, keeping or deleting their copy.
- Sketch drawings stay on each computer instead of syncing live. Pictures made from them, like stickers on a Moodboard, still sync.
- Everyone working together needs version 0.6.0.
