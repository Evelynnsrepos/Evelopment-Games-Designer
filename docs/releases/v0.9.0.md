## What's new in 0.9.0

**Spreadsheet**
- A new **Spreadsheet** tool that works like Excel: type values or formulas like `=SUM(A1:A10)`, `=VLOOKUP(...)` or `=IF(...)`. Hundreds of Excel functions are built in (powered by the open-source HyperFormula engine).
- Several sheets per spreadsheet, with formulas across them (`=Sheet2!A1`). Click or drag over cells while writing a formula to put their address in, even on another sheet.
- Drag the fill handle to continue a series: numbers keep their step, dates count on, "Wave 1" becomes "Wave 2", month and weekday names keep going, and formulas move their references along. Double-click it to fill down, or use Ctrl+D and Ctrl+R.
- Formatting: bold, italic, underline, text and fill colors, alignment, number formats (number, percent, currency, date, text) with decimals, and borders in three thicknesses and any color.
- Insert and delete rows and columns (formulas follow), resize them, sort a selection, copy and paste to and from other programs, and see the sum, average and count of a selection.
- **Damage Calculator results in cells**: drag a Damage Calculator preset onto a cell, or pick one from Calculator in the toolbar. Any of its inputs can come from other cells, so one formula can run for every row of a table.

**Writing Principles**
- A new **Writing Principles** tool with rules for good stories and game writing, each explained and many with an example: show, don't tell; setup and payoff; information theory; save the player from themselves; story and gameplay should agree; and more.
- Story structures as numbered steps: the Hero's Journey, three-act structure, story circle, Save the Cat beats, Kishōtenketsu, seven-point structure and Freytag's pyramid.
- Add your own principles and categories, star the important ones, sort them, and bring back built-in entries you deleted.

**Gameplay Loop**
- A new **Gameplay Loop** tool: build the loop your players repeat as a circular timeline. Each step gets a type, what the player does, what it gives them, how long it takes and notes.
- Size the steps by time to see where the minutes go, and branch notes, variants and ideas off any step, with branches off branches.
