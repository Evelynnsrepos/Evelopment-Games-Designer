## What's new in 0.10.1

- **Mac download is back**: the macOS build of 0.10.0 failed while making the disk image, so 0.10.0 had no `.dmg`. 0.10.1 has it again, and the build now retries that step once if it fails. Everything in [0.10.0](https://github.com/Evelynnsrepos/Evelopment-Games-Designer/releases/tag/v0.10.0) is included.
- **Privacy policy**: a [privacy policy](https://github.com/Evelynnsrepos/Evelopment-Games-Designer/blob/main/PRIVACY.md) in English and a [Datenschutzerklärung](https://github.com/Evelynnsrepos/Evelopment-Games-Designer/blob/main/DATENSCHUTZ.md) in German. In short: no account, no telemetry, no tracking, and your projects stay on your computer. Find it under Help → Privacy policy.
- **Work together** now says clearly that n0's lookup and relay servers see your IP address and a random device ID. Project contents stay end-to-end encrypted.
