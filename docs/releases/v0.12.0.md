## What's new in 0.12.0: Everything connected

Still no generative AI: every idea and every stroke is yours.

**Connections**
- Every item, character, town and enemy page shows Jump to (what it points at, like the items an enemy drops) and Used in (every place in the project that mentions it). One click takes you there.
- Back and forward through your jumps, with the arrows under the project name or Alt+Left and Alt+Right.
- Link to… on any entry links it to another entry, a document or a tool. The link shows on both sides.
- Map zones (Z): mark the land of a faction, a kingdom or a forest. A zone takes its faction's name and color and can link to characters, towns, documents and more.

**Draw** (the Sketch tool has a new name)
- Fixed: when Draw shared the screen with another tool, the canvas could jump while saving, which drew stray lines and broke stabilization.
- Pointer prediction is off while smoothing is on, so no loose line runs ahead of the pen.
- Shortcuts now sits in the corner of the canvas (or press Shift+?).
- Every drawing stays in the project: wherever you can add a picture, a window shows your drawings to pick from first.

**Writer**
- Fonts and sizes for any text.
- Images: left, center, right or with the text flowing around them, 25 to 100% wide, or drag the corner to any size.

**Everywhere**
- A font for each tool: click the small T in a tool's header.
- Add tool has a search field: type to filter, Enter adds the first match.
- The spell helper checks the paragraphs around where you are writing first, so suggestions no longer fall behind in long texts, and it reads the sentences around a mistake for better options.
- A third optional local model, Best (4.7 GB), for the most careful checks. Like the others, it runs on your computer only.
