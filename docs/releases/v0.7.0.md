## What's new in 0.7.0

**New tools**
- **Wave Planner**: plan the waves of a wave or tower-defense mode. Enemies from your Enemy List or custom ones, how many per wave, boss waves every few waves, health growth by percent, flat or by level, and expected drops. A chart and a wave-by-wave table show the damage per second each wave needs, and you can check which weapons keep up.
- **Quest Designer**: quests with a type, quest giver, place, level, objectives and rewards. Link quests into chains and see them as a graph; each quest shows what the chain up to it gives.
- **Dialogue Editor**: branching conversations as a graph with speakers, player choices and lines that nothing leads to marked. Play a conversation through like the player would, and export it as JSON, Yarn Spinner or Ink.
- **Flags** shared by quests and dialogue: values like `met_the_king` or `reputation` that lines, choices and quests can depend on and change.
- **Gacha & Loot Simulator**: tiers and rates (or your project rarities), hard and soft pity, 50/50s with a guarantee, and thousands of simulated players showing how many pulls and how much money a goal takes. Plus chests and drop tables: chance per opening and how many openings a 90% chance takes.
- **Reviews**: give any tool window or entry a status from Idea to Final and comment on it. On the Brainstorm Board, Moodboard, Design Language and Map, the new Comment tool pins comments to a spot. The Reviews board shows everything that waits for review or has open comments.

**Everywhere**
- **Search with Ctrl+K** (Cmd+K on a Mac): find any tool, document, item, character, town, enemy or wiki article, or run a command.
- **Design Book** at the bottom of the sidebar: pick parts of your project and get one game design document with a cover, contents, images and info boxes, as a web page or printed to PDF.
- **The AI helper checks grammar as you write**: wrong words for the sentence, verb forms, missing commas. Its suggestions get a blue underline with one-click fixes. Spell check and the AI helper now also work in every description and notes box, not only in the Writer and Wiki.
- The Work together window now explains what the others can see (your IP address) and that relayed connections go through servers of n0 in the US, still end-to-end encrypted.
- Everyone working together needs version 0.7.0.
