# Privacy Policy – Evelopment Games Designer

*Last updated: 4 October 2026* · [Deutsche Fassung: DATENSCHUTZ.md](DATENSCHUTZ.md)

## The short version

Evelopment Games Designer is a desktop app that works **offline**. It has **no account, no telemetry, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting**. Your projects are saved only on your own computer, in the folder you choose. We, the people who make the app, **never receive or see your data**.

The app only connects to the internet when **you** use one of the features listed below.

## Who is responsible

The app is published by: **Evelynn Müller, Evelynn.mu@proton.me** (Evelopment Games).

Because the app sends us nothing, we do not process personal data about you when you use it. This text explains what the app does on your computer and which other services it can contact when you ask it to.

## What stays on your computer

- **Projects** (text, images, audio, drawings, settings of each project) are saved as files in the project folder you pick.
- **App settings** (theme, window and list preferences, which tips you have seen, your brushes, installed plugins) are saved in the app's data folder on your computer.
- **Spell check** works with dictionaries that ship with the app. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **"Ask your project" and AI grammar help** are optional and **fully local**. They run on your computer, work offline once installed, do not send your text anywhere, and nothing is used to train any AI. They only look things up in what you wrote; they never write stories for you.

You can delete all of this at any time by deleting the project folder and uninstalling the app.

## When the app connects to the internet

Only when you start one of these actions:

| What you do | Who the app contacts | What they can see |
|---|---|---|
| Turn on the local AI helper (one-time download) | Hugging Face (AI model file) and GitHub (llama.cpp program) | Your IP address and that the file was downloaded. Your text is never sent. |
| Install a plugin from a GitHub link | GitHub (github.com, api.github.com, codeload.github.com) | Your IP address and which plugin you downloaded. |
| Work together directly (peer to peer) | The people you share with; when needed, public relay and lookup servers run by n0 (a US company, the makers of iroh) | Teammates see your IP address on direct connections. n0 sees your IP address and a random device ID. Project contents are end-to-end encrypted, so n0 cannot read them. |
| Work through an Evelopment Games Designer Server | The server named in your connect code | That server stores the project and the name you type. Whoever runs that server is responsible for it and must give you their own privacy notice. Only use servers you trust. |
| Click a link (GitHub, Discord, YouTube, Instagram, report a problem, or links you added) | The website, opened in your normal browser | The usual information a website receives. Their own privacy policies apply. |

The app itself does not check for updates in the background and makes no other connections.

## Data you share with teammates

When you work together, your teammates receive a full copy of the shared project and the name you type for yourself. They can keep that copy. Only share with people you trust.

## Downloading the app

The app is downloaded from GitHub Releases (or a package source such as the AUR). GitHub, Inc. processes the usual connection data for that download under its own privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

## Contacting us

If you write to us (by e-mail, on Discord, or in a GitHub issue), we use what you send only to answer you. Messages on Discord and GitHub are also processed by those platforms under their own policies. GitHub issues are public, so please do not post personal data there.

## Your rights (GDPR)

If we do hold personal data about you (for example because you e-mailed us), you have the right to access, correction, deletion, restriction, data portability and to object (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Contact us at Evelynn.mu@proton.me. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, for example the one in your state of residence.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If the app's behavior changes (for example a new online feature), this policy will be updated and the change will be listed in the release notes.
